DRONE STRIKE ORBIT

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-10

DRONE STRIKE ORBIT does not itself collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Game progress and purchase entitlement state are stored locally on the device.

Advertising

The app shows optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier), ad interaction data, and diagnostic data. On iOS, the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission; if you decline, ads remain available but are not personalized. Ad data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchasing the one-time "Remove Ads" product disables all advertising in the app.

Purchases

App Store in-app purchase processing is handled by Apple. Google Play in-app purchase processing is handled by Google Play. Purchase records and payment information are managed by the relevant store provider under its own policies.

What the app does not include

The app does not include analytics SDKs other than the advertising SDK described above, account registration, chat, user-generated content, location tracking, contact access, camera access, or microphone access.
